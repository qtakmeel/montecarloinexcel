Why a spreadsheet

Black boxes teach nothing.

Most Monte Carlo courses hand you Python notebooks you will never open again, or add-ins that do the work invisibly. This toolkit takes the opposite road: the simulation is the spreadsheet. That is how you build the intuition that lasts: randomness is not a black box, it is a loop you can read.
